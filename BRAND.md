# The Grand Tour, in gold and pearl

Brand guide for Sandra's 50th and Bray's 30th, Oct 16 to 29, 2027. Picked Oct 30, 2026 from the directions board (direction 3).

## The idea

Two birthdays, one week apart, at either end of a trip across Europe. The route is the logo: **Paris in gold** for Sandra's 50th (Thu Oct 21), **Tuscany in pearl** for Bray's 30th (Thu Oct 28). Gold for 50 and pearl for 30 are the traditional anniversary metals. The Grand Tour was the old coming-of-age route through Paris, over the Alps and down to Florence, which is almost exactly ours.

## Name

- Full lockup, stacked: *The* (italic) / **GRAND TOUR** / *in gold and pearl* (italic).
- In a sentence: "the Grand Tour". In a link title: "The Grand Tour · 50 and 30".
- Never "Grand Tour 2027" or "GT". The year, when needed, is MMXXVII in small caps.

## Marks

- **The route:** a 1 px gold line with four stops, Amsterdam · Paris · Lucerne · Tuscany. Paris is a solid gold disc and Tuscany a solid pearl disc, each twice the size of the small stops. Amsterdam and Lucerne are small outlined dots. City names sit above in Cinzel caps; the birthday lines sit below in italic ("Fifty, Oct 21" in gold, "Thirty, Oct 28" in pearl).
- **The badge:** a gold diamond outline with **50** in gold over a short gold rule over **30** in pearl. Use it for the favicon, the invitation, and anywhere the full name doesn't fit.
- **The frame:** two 1 px gold rules 8 px apart, for the hero and for cards on midnight.

## Color

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Midnight | `#141A2E` | Grounds: hero, cards, link preview |
| Gold | `#C9A45C` | Rules, the Paris stop, accents. Gold **text** only on midnight |
| Pearl | `#F3EEE4` | Text on midnight, page ground, the Tuscany stop |
| Slate | `#3A4566` | Labels and links on pearl |
| Mist | `#D9D3C7` | Secondary text on midnight, rules on pearl |
| Muted | `#555261` | Secondary text on pearl |

Contrast: gold on midnight is about 7.4:1, slate on pearl about 7.9:1. Gold on pearl fails for text, so use it on pearl only for rules, dots and fills.

## Type

- **Cinzel** (500, letter-spaced 0.04 to 0.18 em): the name, headings, city names, labels, prices. Capitals only.
- **Cormorant Garamond** (400 and 500, plus italic): everything else. Italic in gold on midnight for the accents ("in gold and pearl", "Fifty, Oct 21").
- Google Fonts: `family=Cinzel:wght@400;500;600&family=Cormorant+Garamond:ital,wght@0,400;0,500;0,600;1,400;1,500`.
- Body at least 18 px on phones (Cormorant runs small).

## Voice

- "We", from Sandra and Bray. Plain and short, like the Breckenridge app.
- **No em or en dashes.** Use "to", commas or periods ("October 16 to 29, 2027").
- Money: "about $3,050 each", "+$370". It's a price, never "what you owe".
- Sign-off: "Love, Sandra and Bray".
